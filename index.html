Our Story

A Table Under the Sky

Alfresco Pizzeria & Bistro was built around a love for good food and laid-back dining. Inspired by the joy of sharing meals with family and friends, we created a space where comfort, flavor, and hospitality come together.

From freshly prepared pizzas to satisfying house favorites, every dish is made with care using quality ingredients. Whether you're here for casual dinners, celebrations, or late-night cravings, Alfresco is made to feel welcoming, familiar, and worth coming back to.
